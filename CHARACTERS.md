# Indigo – Characters

Indigo has eight playable characters, each with a different moveset and set of mechanics.

## General Gameplay

These mechanics are shared by all characters unless a character's section says otherwise.

- **Jump:** Every character can jump from the ground and perform one additional jump in the air.
- **Dash:** Every character has an air dash. It refreshes when they land.
- **Parry:** Every character can parry an incoming attack. A successful parry reflects projectiles or stuns the attacker, and restores your jump and dash charges.
- **Riposte:** After parrying an opponent, pressing Neutral Light while they are nearby performs a riposte.
- **Directional attacks:** Hold a direction while pressing Light or Heavy to choose a move. Press Light or Heavy without a direction for a Neutral attack.
- **Custom controls:** Keyboard and controller bindings can be changed in the Controls settings.

Some characters have exceptions to the shared movement rules. These are listed below.

## Ven

*Gunslinger*

### Passives & Extras

- Has **2 special-ammo charges**.
- Heavy attacks consume one charge.
- Ammo is fully replenished when Ven touches the ground.

### Light

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Punch** – A quick close-range punch. A nearby opponent who has just been parried can be hit with a Riposte instead. |
| Up | **Volley** – Fires an upward blast that launches opponents high. |
| Down (grounded) | **Breaker** – A close-range attack with high damage and stun. |
| Down (aerial) | **Ground Slam** – Slams down, then attacks on both sides when Ven lands. |
| Side | **Blast** – A short-range horizontal attack with upward knockback. |

### Heavy

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Triple Shot** – Fires three bullets in a spread: upward, straight ahead and downward. |
| Up | **Blast Bouncer** – Fires downward and launches Ven upwards. |
| Down | **Rifle Shot** – Fires a powerful, long-range rifle shot. |
| Side | **Back Blast** – Fires behind Ven and launches him in the opposite direction. |

## Rend

*Cursed Swordsman*

### Passives & Extras

- **Consume** can be charged and stored. The longer it is charged, the stronger and larger its eventual burst.
- Getting hit while charging Consume interrupts it and loses the charge.

### Light

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Stab** – A forward sword strike with extra upward angle. Can become a Riposte after a successful parry. |
| Up | **Poke** – An upward sword strike. |
| Down (grounded) | **Jab** – A slower, close-range strike that launches almost straight upwards. |
| Down (aerial) | **Pogo** – A downward slash that bounces Rend upwards if it hits an opponent. |
| Side | **Slash** – A forward slash with slight upward knockback. |

### Heavy

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Consume** – Hold Heavy to charge, press Heavy again to store the charge, then press Neutral Heavy again to release a radial burst. |
| Up | **Frenzy Dive** – Rises with an attack before diving down into a powerful slam. Hitting an opponent during the dive bounces Rend back upwards. |
| Down (grounded) | **Pierce** – A powerful forward thrust. |
| Down (aerial) | **Aerial Pierce** – The aerial version of Pierce. |
| Side | **Throw Stone** – Throws a stone that pulls opponents towards Rend when it hits. |

## Cleaning Lady

*Long-reaching mop attacks, a squirt bottle and a trash bag.*

### Passives & Extras

- Dealing damage fills the **Trash Bag**.
- The bag can hold up to 100 trash.
- Once full, Down Heavy empties it in a much larger and stronger spray.
- The bag briefly stops gaining trash after being emptied.

### Light

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Sweep Dash** – Steps forward and performs a short sweep. Can become a Riposte after a successful parry. |
| Up | **Pole Up** – Strikes directly above with the mop. |
| Down (grounded) | **Soap** – Throws a fast, weak soap projectile. |
| Down (aerial) | **Stick Down** – Thrusts the mop straight down. |
| Side | **Pole Side** – A long, thin strike straight ahead. |

### Heavy

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Squirt** – Sprays seven small droplets over a short distance. |
| Up | **Pole Grapple** – Throws a pole upwards and pulls Cleaning Lady towards an opponent it hits. |
| Down | **Trash Bag** – Empties a full trash bag in a powerful spray. Requires a full bag. |
| Side | **Lunge** – A forward mop lunge with a long recovery. |

## Cap'n Rex

*Cutlass fighter with a throwable barrel.*

### Passives & Extras

- Rex has a **barrel** that can be picked up, carried, thrown, jumped with and slammed.
- Several moves change depending on whether Rex is holding the barrel.
- If the barrel is lost off-stage, it takes 5 seconds to return.

### Light

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Lunge Grab / Throw Barrel** – If Rex is not holding the barrel, lunges towards it to pick it up. If he is holding it, throws it. Can become a Riposte after a successful parry when not carrying the barrel. |
| Up | **Cutlass Up** – An upward cutlass swing. |
| Down (grounded) | **Tentacles** – Summons three tentacles in front of Rex, launching opponents upwards. Has a long recovery. |
| Down (aerial) | **Cutlass Down** – A downward cutlass swing. |
| Side | **Blade Wave / Throw Barrel** – Sends a wave of blade energy forward, or throws the barrel if Rex is holding it. |

### Heavy

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Barrel Jump** – Makes the barrel jump. If Rex is holding it, he jumps with it. |
| Up | **Barrel Dash** – Dashes towards the barrel. If carrying it, Rex throws it upwards and follows it. |
| Down | **Barrel Slam** – Slams the barrel, or slams down while carrying it, creating shockwaves. |
| Side | **Ship** – Sends a ship across the screen at Rex's height. The barrel can ride along if Rex is holding it. |

## Bombhead

*Explosives fighter with a ticking fuse.*

### Passives & Extras

- Bombhead has a **30-second fuse**.
- When the fuse runs out, Bombhead self-destructs.
- The fuse resets when another player is eliminated and when Bombhead respawns.
- Up Heavy can only be used a limited number of times before landing.

### Light

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Throw Bomb** – Throws a small bomb that bounces once before exploding. Can become a Riposte after a successful parry. |
| Up | **Upswing** – A straightforward upward attack. |
| Down | **Slide Kick** – Slides forward with a kick. In the air, Bombhead first slams down. |
| Side | **Bomb Dash** – Dashes towards opponents. |

### Heavy

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Haymaker** – A very slow wind-up followed by a powerful horizontal hit. |
| Up | **Blast Launch** – An explosion launches Bombhead upwards and away. |
| Down | **Drop Bomb** – Drops a bomb from above, in front of Bombhead. |
| Side | **Shell** – Fires a slow explosive projectile that detonates when it hits a player. |

## Angellica

*Low-gravity fighter who uses light-based abilities.*

### Passives & Extras

- Has **reduced gravity**.
- Has **2 air dashes** instead of the usual 1.
- Uses a resource called **Light**, with a maximum of 3 charges.
- Certain attacks can restore Light when they hit while Angellica is airborne.
- Her Up Heavy can be used once before touching the ground again.

### Light

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Orbs** – Summons three orbs that rise, track opponents, pause and then fire. Can become a Riposte after a successful parry. |
| Up | **Column** – Creates a column of light directly above Angellica. |
| Down | **Wings** – Releases an attack outwards on both sides. |
| Side | **Grab Slam** – Dashes forward to grab an opponent and slam them down. |

### Heavy

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Gravity Zone** – Creates an area where gravity is reduced for everyone. Costs 1 Light. |
| Up | **Ascend** – Prepares and surges upwards. Free, but limited to once per landing. |
| Down | **Light Pillar** – Creates a lingering column of light in front of Angellica. Costs 1 Light. |
| Side | **Starball** – Sends out a ball that slows down before creating a large, growing explosion. Costs all 3 Light. |

## The Watcher

*Portal and tentacle-based fighter.*

### Passives & Extras

- Can place **two portals**, one at a time.
- Once both portals are placed, they can be used to travel across the stage.
- Portals can be retrieved and placed again, but each has a short cooldown after being retrieved.
- Up Heavy can only be used once before landing again.

### Light

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Floor Tentacle** – A tentacle bursts out of the floor in front of The Watcher and spikes upwards. Can become a Riposte after a successful parry. |
| Up | **Big Swipe** – A large upward-angled swipe in front of The Watcher. |
| Down | **Sky Tentacle** – A tentacle appears above and in front of The Watcher, striking downwards. |
| Side | **Tentacle Barrage** – Three tentacles lash out sideways after a long startup. |

### Heavy

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Portal One** – Places or retrieves the first portal. |
| Up | **Tentacle Lift** – A tentacle underneath The Watcher launches them upwards. Limited to once per landing. |
| Down | **Portal Two** – Places or retrieves the second portal. |
| Side | **Tentacle Ball** – Throws a ball that sprouts four tentacles shortly after. |

## Sion

*Vampire who trades health for power.*

### Passives & Extras

- Uses **Pain** to increase his damage.
- Pain is gained by sacrificing health through Pain Trade.
- Each point of Pain increases damage by 1%, up to a maximum of 200 Pain (double damage).
- Pain can also be spent to restore health, with a limit of 50 health restored per stock.
- At maximum Pain, Sion gains **4 air jumps** instead of the usual 1.
- Pain resets when Sion respawns.

### Light

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Hook Slash** – A forward slash that pulls the opponent towards Sion. Can become a Riposte after a successful parry. |
| Up | **Upper Slice** – A simple upward slash. |
| Down | **Low Slice** – A low forward slice. If it hits, a delayed follow-up slash appears on the opponent. |
| Side | **Slash** – A standard forward slash. |

### Heavy

| Direction | Move |
|---|---|
| Neutral | **Pain Trade** – Choose how much health to trade for Pain, then press Heavy again to confirm. |
| Up | **Shadow Rise** – Creates a hitbox above Sion, then teleports him upwards into it. |
| Down | **Blood Fountain** – Sends six blood drops upwards before they rain back down. |
| Side | **Bat Teleport** – Vanishes and reappears ahead, with three bats attacking along the route. |
